Privacy

Effective September 23, 2026

Current production behavior: COMPASS does not require an account, does not run advertising analytics, and does not intentionally maintain a COMPASS-owned history of your precise location. Nearby-business discovery prefers a COMPASS-controlled gateway using a coarse search center; exact distance and bearing calculations remain in your browser.

Location

Your browser may provide foreground location after you grant permission. COMPASS uses that location in the browser to calculate distance and bearing and to request nearby map data. Location permission can be revoked through your browser or operating-system settings.

Local storage

The app may store theme preference, provider configuration, cached points of interest, and recent search metadata in your browser so it can start faster and degrade more gracefully when connectivity is poor.

External map infrastructure

COMPASS uses OpenStreetMap tiles for maps. Nearby-business discovery prefers the COMPASS API, which receives a search center rounded to approximately 0.02 degrees rather than the browser's exact GPS fix, then queries OpenStreetMap/Overpass data. Exact distance and bearing are recomputed locally in the browser. If the controlled API is unavailable, the application may fall back to public Overpass infrastructure for continuity; that fallback can receive the coordinates needed to perform the search. External infrastructure is governed by its own policies and may receive network metadata such as your IP address.

Accounts, payments, and sales

The current consumer application has no user account system and does not sell cannabis, process cannabis payments, or place cannabis orders.

Future services

COMPASS now operates a first-party POI gateway for coarse nearby searches. Merchant accounts, advertising analytics, billing, or other additional data processing are not part of the current consumer service. This notice will be updated before those features become part of production.

Questions

Use the project repository's public contact channels for privacy questions.
